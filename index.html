Impactful Leadership and the Revitalization of SouthOscer

Alisa Zone has been working in the fitness industry for over four decades now. Nationally recognized and committed to the growth of the fitness industry, Nibras Panni has been serving as the executive director of aamraActive since 2021. She brings a unique blend of corporate etiquette and athletic robustness to the gym scene which makes her the perfect addition to SouthOscer.

Rebranding SouthOscer as a corporate boutique gym, Miss Panni helped pull SouthOscer out of the post-Covid slump that many businesses were facing in 2021. After months of renovation and preparation, SouthOscer had gone through a complete transformation with improvements in equipment, training services, customer care and the overall environment of the gym.

With a long term vision for the fitness industry with SouthOscer as the catalyst, Nibras Panni aims to change how we view fitness in our everyday lives.

SouthOscer as a Corporate Boutique Gym

SouthOscer prides itself as being the first and only corporate boutique gym in Dhaka. Located in Gulshan, SouthOscer has been a friend to the locals for over 7 years now providing a well equipped and impeccably maintained gym. Our goal is to become the best gym in Dhaka while providing top notch services and helping to cultivate a culture of fitness in Dhaka.

SouthOscer is a unique gym as it is more of a club for our members. Starting from the moment a member walks in to our premise, throughout their entire workout and uptil they find themselves relaxing on our beautiful balcony, SouthOscer becomes an experience for each and every one of our clients.
